Reticulum is here to **learn**, not to run camp comms. It's a cryptography-based
networking stack that runs over almost anything — LoRa, packet radio, Wi-Fi, TCP — with
strong encryption built in and no addresses to register. At camp it's **workshops only**:
come build something; no production traffic is expected.

It does **not bridge** to Meshcore or Meshtastic — it's a parallel world. Curious hackers
welcome; if you just want on the camp mesh, that's [Meshcore](@/meshcore/_index.md).
