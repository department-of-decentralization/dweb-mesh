Meshtastic is **supported, not run by us.** You can flash it and use the camp's
Meshtastic mesh, but the camp feeds it **no services** — no bots, no AI host, no schedule
announcements. Those all live on [Meshcore](@/meshcore/_index.md), the primary stack.

A Meshtastic **repeater sits next to the Meshcore repeater** on the beer barn chimney for
coverage — that is the full extent of our involvement. The stacks are **parallel and do
not bridge**: a Meshtastic node cannot see Meshcore or Reticulum traffic.
