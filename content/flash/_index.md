> Already running the latest <span class="hi">Meshcore</span> firmware? Move on to → [Config](@/config/_index.md).

Flash your device below.

1. Choose your device: **Elecrow ThinkNode M1**, **Seeed Studio SenseCAP T1000-E**, **Heltec v4**, or your own device if you brought one.
2. Choose **Companion Bluetooth** for the ThinkNode M1 or Seeed T1000-E. Choose **Companion USB** for the Heltec v4.
3. Select the latest version.
4. Enter DFU mode (ThinkNode and T1000-E only).
5. Flash.
