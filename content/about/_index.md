A network that can't be rebuilt from its own documentation is a single point of failure.

This site is the **portable memory of the camp mesh**: how to join it, how to flash for
it, where to go when the radio goes silent. It's built to survive with **no upstream
internet** — every byte ships in the bundle, so it works off a laptop, off the Freifunk
wifi, off a USB stick passed hand to hand.

We run three stacks, parallel and unbridged: **Meshcore** (primary — where the services
live), **Meshtastic** (supported, no services from us), and **Reticulum** (educational,
for the workshops). Pick one and go. No gateways, no magic interop, no cloud.

This is our own thing — not a brand, not a product, not DWeb-official. Just the people
keeping the camp talking when the towers don't. If you're reading this on the mesh with
the internet down: it's working.

> Lost? **Mesh Nest, tent 5.** Radio dark? **Matrix — #dweb-mesh:dod.ngo** (in the footer
> of every page). That's how you reach a human.
