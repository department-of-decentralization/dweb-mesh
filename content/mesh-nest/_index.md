The **Mesh Nest** is the helpdesk for everything on this site. Can't flash, can't join,
radio's gone dark — bring it here.

## Where & when
- **Location:** **tent 5** on the camp map.
- **Hours:** **TBC** — posted at the tent and on the [Agenda](@/agenda/_index.md).

## Watch the mesh from outside
We monitor the camp mesh with **PotatoMesh** — a federated Meshtastic **and** Meshcore
dashboard with a live map and chat: nodes, positions, neighbours, trace routes, telemetry
and messages. Good for spotting dead spots and confirming the chimney repeater is up.

- **Camp dashboard: <https://dweb.potatomesh.net>**
- Project / federation: <https://potatomesh.net> · <https://github.com/l5yth/potato-mesh>

## When LoRa itself is down
The escape hatch is **Matrix** — it does not need the mesh:

> **#dweb-mesh:dod.ngo** → <https://matrix.to/#/#dweb-mesh:dod.ngo>

When the radio fails, that's how you reach a human. It's in the footer of every page, too.
