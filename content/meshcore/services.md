Services the camp runs **on Meshcore** — and only here. Meshtastic gets none from our
side, and the stacks don't bridge.

## Chimney repeater
A Meshcore **repeater** sits on the **beer barn chimney** for camp-wide coverage. It
uses Meshcore's structured routing (not blind rebroadcast), so it extends range without
flooding the mesh. If your node can see it, you're on.

## blackbox — local AI host
A **local AI host** ("blackbox"), Meshcore-tailored and reachable over the mesh: message
it like any contact and answers come back over LoRa. It runs **locally — no internet**.
_How to query it, and how much of its internals are public: **TBC** (team-supplied)._

## Schedule announcements
